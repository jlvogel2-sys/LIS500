Team Members

Josie Vogel, Raneem Yasin, and Plangkat Rinkam

Josie Vogel

Hi everyone, my name is Josie and I'm a junior majoring in Information Science with a certificate in Data Science. Some of my hobbies are playing soccer, watercolor painting, and hiking in nature. Some of the projects I've been working on recently are exploring the intersection of education and AI tools for multilingual learners. I took this class hoping to learn more about how different identities have been promoted or hidden in the creation of the tech industry. Coming from Silicon Valley, learning about the origins of tech culture has been very eye-opening. My favorite technology is my smartphone because it keeps me connected to my friends and family. My tech hero is Meredith Whittaker, the president of the Signal Foundation.
