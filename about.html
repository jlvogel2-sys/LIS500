Project Overview

Our initiative focuses on the intersection of human-computer interaction (HCI), artificial intelligence, and social justice. As AI tools are increasingly deployed across educational institutions and public services, ensuring they operate fairly across diverse demographic groups is paramount.

Our research focuses on evaluating automated tools for multilingual fairness, red-teaming AI systems against harmful stereotypes, and building grounded models that assist educators without replacing human oversight.

Core Research Pillars

1. Multilingual Fairness

Evaluating natural language models to ensure non-native English speakers receive equitable evaluation and assistance without dialectal bias.

2. Adversarial Safety & Testing

Stress-testing language agents to identify vulnerabilities, prompt injection risks, and stereotyping before tools reach vulnerable user populations.

3. Curriculum-Grounded AI

Designing retrieval systems grounded in verified educational content to keep AI responses accurate, reliable, and trustworthy.
