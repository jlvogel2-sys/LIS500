Welcome

Welcome to our research portal. This website serves as an educational clearinghouse focused on human-centered artificial intelligence, algorithmic fairness, and equitable technology practices.

Through our work, we examine how automated systems impact diverse communities, provide accessible learning resources on implicit bias for students, and highlight emerging leaders driving equity in the tech sector.

Key Focus Areas

  • Educational Equity: Developing inclusive AI tools for multilingual and neurodivergent learners.
  • Public Resources: Curating accessible guides on implicit bias and intersectionality for first-year college students.
