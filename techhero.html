Dr. Rediet Abebe

Dr. Rediet Abebe is a computer scientist specializing in algorithms and artificial intelligence. She was born and raised in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. She is the first Black woman to earn a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Cornell! She is currently an assistant professor of computer science at the University of California, Berkeley.

She co-founded Mechanism Design for Social Good (MD4SG) alongside Kira Goldner in 2016. The goals of MD4SG is to use algorithms and mechanism design to tackle inequality. Her work with MD4SG had her honored as a pioneer in the 2019 MIT Technology Review's Innovators Under 35. She also co-founded Black in AI, a network of 1,500 researchers working on AI, with Timnit Gebru, in 2016.
