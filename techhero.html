Spotlight: Dr. Rediet Abebe

Role: Assistant Professor of Computer Science at UC Berkeley & Co-Founder of MD4SG

Dr. Rediet Abebe is a Ethiopian-born computer scientist whose groundbreaking work bridges artificial intelligence, algorithms, and social equity. After becoming the first Black woman to earn a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Cornell University, Dr. Abebe focused her research on using algorithms and optimization to improve access and outcomes for historically marginalized communities.

Dr. Abebe co-founded Mechanism Design for Social Good (MD4SG), an international multi-disciplinary research initiative that applies mathematical and algorithmic frameworks to address social problems like poverty, housing instability, and healthcare access. Furthermore, she co-founded Black in AI, an organization dedicated to increasing representation, mentorship, and research opportunities for Black scholars in artificial intelligence globally.

Her work directly challenges the tech industry's status quo by demonstrating that algorithms should not merely maximize profit or efficiency, but actively serve the public good and reduce systemic inequality.
